04 — A Letter For You

My love,

I honestly don't know if words will ever be enough to explain what these four years with you mean to me.

We've had beautiful days, silly moments, arguments, random conversations, laughter, and moments that only you and I could understand.

But if there is one thing I know for sure, it's that having you in my life has made my life better.

I love you not only for the beautiful moments, but also for the ordinary ones. The random calls. The stupid jokes. The late-night conversations. The little fights that eventually make us laugh again. The moments when we don't even need words.

You have seen different versions of me. You've seen my good days and my bad ones, and somehow, you're still here.

And I hope you always know how special that is to me.

I don't need everything in life to be perfect. I just want the important things to stay. And you are one of those things.

Thank you for being you. Thank you for being a part of my life. And thank you for making these four years some of the most beautiful years of my life.

I love you. More than these words can say.

— Yours, always ♡
